Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Information we collect

SmartNotes collects account details such as your email address, display name, and authentication provider. Study notes and messages you submit are stored so the student exchange can provide its features.

2. Firebase services

When configured, Firebase Authentication handles sign-in and Firebase Firestore stores profiles, note metadata, file chunks, and conversations. Firebase may process data according to Google's service terms and privacy documentation.

3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate students, sync notes, enable downloads and chats, maintain security, and improve the learning experience. We do not sell student notes or personal information.

4. Local fallback

When Firebase is unavailable, the demo mode stores sessions, note metadata, and chats in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB. Clearing browser data removes that local information.

5. Your choices

You can stop using the service, sign out, or request removal of content through the project maintainers. Do not upload sensitive personal information into study notes or chat messages.
