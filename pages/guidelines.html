Student Guidelines & Academic Integrity

Build a useful, respectful knowledge exchange for every campus.

Share learning, not answers

Upload your own explanations, annotated lecture decks, summaries, and study guides. Never share active exams, answer keys, paid course materials, or content intended to bypass an assessment.

Respect classmates

Keep chats constructive and course-focused. Do not harass, impersonate, dox, spam, or share private information. Message classmates only when your conversation is welcome.

Attribute and verify

Credit professors, authors, and classmates where appropriate. Label your notes accurately, avoid claiming another person's work as your own, and flag uncertain information for review.

Report problems

Report harmful, infringing, or academically dishonest content to the SmartNotes maintainers. Content that violates these guidelines may be removed and accounts may lose access.

Be a good study partner

Use SmartNotes to understand concepts, compare approaches, and support peers. When in doubt, follow the stricter rule from your university or course instructor.
